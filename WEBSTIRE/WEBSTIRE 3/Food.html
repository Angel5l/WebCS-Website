Food

Within culture, the culinary arts showcase the desires and beliefs within it.
Ideas such as Coffee are masculine, whilst tea is more feminine. These types of comparisons and analogies were present during this era.
Thus, as a person who wants to form one’s lifestyle to that of the Victorians, what resources are available to learn from?

The Victorian Way

The Victorian Way not only showcase the recipes found within the Victorian Era, but also live in it.
They discuss lives and people as if you had just time traveled back into the past.
Throughout their performance, they do provide historical and cultural insights of the time by using integrating such knowledge within their dialogues.

Tasting History with Max Miller

Tasting History with Max Miller covers an array of topics throughout the years, not just the Victorian Era.
So, if you are looking to broaden your scope of the type of lifestyle you want, they can provide you with the information.
Here, Max discusses more on the history and acts more of a teacher than the previous’ performative display.
He gives information while the food he prepared is cooking, which is quite nice.
