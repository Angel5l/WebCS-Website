Forming Victorian

Live Life with Regality

Curiosity, Intrigue, Fascination

With the theatrics out of the way, if you want to form your life into that of a Victorian, you have come to the right place.
Sources from books to more contemporary videos are recommended for those just starting to explore the beautiful world of the old
(Without the imperialism, classism, racism, and other bad stuff)
