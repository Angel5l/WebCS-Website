Etiquette

Rules and Culture go hand in hand, they dictate the lives of the people within them.
Therefore, if you want to have a bit of limitations and nuances in your life, here are some resources to do so.

The Ladies Book of Etiquette and Manual for Politeness

This is a hefty book with a lot of intricacies.
It covers the entirety of a mannerisms of a Lady within the time period.
From how to dine, to dressing, to socializing, it is essential for those who take this lifestyle seriously.

The Ladies Book of Etiquette and Manual for Politeness

Now, the previous book was quite expounded and would discourage some of you.
Fear not, for this book is a bite size preview of Victorian Etiquette.
Some do not have the time to read such long texts and would really appreciate a small but dandy guide instead.
